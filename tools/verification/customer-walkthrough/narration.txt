1. Welcome to SAYAND
Welcome to Sayand. In this short guide, we will explore the store, choose a product, understand preorders, and show you where to find checkout, order updates and shopping help.

2. Find your collection
Start at the top menu. Choose Women, Men or Accessories to explore a collection. Click a product to open its page. Select the Sayand logo whenever you want to return to the homepage.

3. Explore the product
On a product page, browse the photographs using the thumbnails or swipe through the gallery. Use the zoom control for a closer look at the fabric, colours and finishing details.

4. Check the fit
Read Description and Details, then open Measurements. Compare the listed measurements with your own or a garment that fits well. Check the available sizes and stock information before choosing your piece.

5. Choose your options
Choose an available size, check the colour, and set the quantity with the plus and minus controls. Then choose Add to Bag. If prompted, sign in to continue shopping with your account.

6. Find preorder drops
Preorder Drops have their own section inside the relevant collection. These are future releases, so always check the estimated dispatch date. A preorder is not the same as an item ready for immediate shipment.

7. Understand preorder pricing
The preorder page shows the intended launch price, the price you pay now, and the saving. You pay in full at checkout. The displayed dispatch date is an estimate for shipping, not a guaranteed delivery date.

8. Sign in to your account
Use the account icon to sign in. Enter your email and password, or choose an available social sign-in option. New customers can select Sign up here. Forgot password helps you recover access.

9. Review your shopping bag
Open Bag in the header after signing in. Review your items, sizes and quantities, and remove anything you no longer want. Check the total, then select Proceed to Checkout when you are ready.

10. Complete checkout
At checkout, enter your contact information and delivery address. Apply a valid promo code if you have one. Review shipping and the final total, then choose Complete Order to continue to secure payment.

11. Follow your order
After purchase, sign in to My Account to view your orders. Preorder items are labelled Preordered. When your order is shipped, its status updates and a shipping email is triggered with tracking information.

12. Ask ELIA for help
For shopping help, click Ask Elia, then Start chatting. Ask about products, sizes or preorders. Product answers can include a picture, details and a View product link. Use My Orders for private order information.

13. Discover the editorial
For inspiration, explore the editorial from the homepage. Browse the looks and stories, then follow product or collection links to discover the pieces behind the styling.

14. Know where to get support
The footer contains Frequently Asked Questions, shipping information, returns and order tracking. Check these pages for current policies, or contact Sayand support when you need help with your own order.

15. You are ready to explore
You are ready to explore Sayand. Browse the collections, check your fit and any preorder dates, then review your bag before paying. Visit the Sayand website to begin.
